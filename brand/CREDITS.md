## Notes for the cut

- Best opener for the bridge shot: `01_bridge_over_destroyed_river.jpg` (aerial, bridge over a brown river) and `07_river_pra_near_central.jpg` (railing POV over the muddy Pra).
- Best before/after: the two Dunkwa satellite frames, same season. Bare-earth scars along the Offin are visible in both and spread clearly by 2026. That's what the imagery shows; it doesn't prove who caused it, so narration should say 'scars' or 'signs of mining', not name culprits.
- Mining-site texture: 03, 04, 12, 13, 14. 12 and 13/14 are low-res (about 1000 px), so use them small or in motion only.
- 05 and 11 are captioned on Commons as Ghana pollution photos without an exact location, so don't caption them as a named river.

## Graded versions
