Step 2 of 3

Film the water

This is your evidence: it shows the river is muddy today.

  • From a bridge, point down at the water
  • From the bank, aim across at open water, not the muddy edge
  • Keep still for about 10 seconds, in daylight
  • Stay somewhere public. Never walk up to a mine

Film about 10 seconds of the water, or take a photo, to continue.
